## Long description
FastDownloader is a free, lightweight download manager for Windows.

• Faster downloads – every file is split into up to 32 parts that download at the same time.
• Pause & resume – even after closing the app or restarting your PC.
• Video downloads from 1,800+ websites – a popup shows only the qualities the video really has
  (4K, 2K, 1080p, 720p, 480p …) with their file sizes, plus audio only (MP3).
• Copy a video link and the quality picker appears by itself.
• Browser extension for Chrome, Brave and Edge – a Download button on videos and automatic
  catching of normal downloads (exe, zip, iso, pdf, mp4 …).
• Saves videos as MP4 that play on any device.
• No ads, no account, no tracking – everything runs on your PC.

Note: DRM-protected streaming services (Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+) are not supported.

### Permission justifications (the store asks for these)
- **downloads** – to hand browser downloads over to the FastDownloader app.
- **contextMenus** – "Download with FastDownloader" in the right-click menu.
- **storage** – remembers the "Catch browser downloads" switch.
- **cookies** – downloads that need you to be signed in keep working; cookies for that one link are given
  to the app on the same computer only.
- **tabs** – reads the current tab's address when you click "Download video on this page".
- **Host permission (all sites)** – shows the Download button on videos on any website and talks to the
  local app at 127.0.0.1.
- **Single purpose:** send downloads from the browser to the FastDownloader download manager.
- **Remote code:** No.
- **Data usage:** no user data is collected or sold.
