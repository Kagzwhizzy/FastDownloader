# FastDownloader – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 17 November 2026*

FastDownloader (the Windows app and the browser extension "FastDownloader – Download Manager" /
"FastDownloader Integration") is built so that **your data stays on your own computer**.

## What the extension does with data

- **Download links, page address and page title** – when you start a download or click the
  *Download* button, the extension sends that link to the FastDownloader app **running on your own PC**
  (address `127.0.0.1`, which never leaves your computer).
- **Cookies for the file you download** – so that downloads which need you to be signed in keep
  working, the extension passes the cookies for *that download link only* to the app on your PC.
  They are used for that one download and are not stored or sent anywhere else.
- **Settings** – the "Catch browser downloads" on/off switch is stored in your browser.

## What we do NOT do

- We do **not** collect, sell or share any personal data.
- We have **no servers**. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.
- No analytics, no tracking, no ads.

## Donations

The *Support* buttons open PayPal's website or show a crypto address. Payments are handled entirely by
PayPal or the blockchain network; the extension and app never see your payment details.
