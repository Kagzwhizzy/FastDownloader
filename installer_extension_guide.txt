Add the FastDownloader button to your browser (1 minute)
=======================================================

Chrome / Brave / Edge:

 1. Open a new tab and go to:
       Chrome:  chrome://extensions
       Brave:   brave://extensions
       Edge:    edge://extensions

 2. Turn ON "Developer mode" (switch in the top-right corner; in Edge it's on the left).

 3. Click "Load unpacked".

 4. Choose the folder:  Browser Extension
    It is inside the FastDownloader install folder
    (Start menu -> FastDownloader -> "Browser Extension folder" opens it).
    Click the folder once, then "Select Folder".

 5. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin FastDownloader.

Done! Now:
 - Play any video -> click the blue "Download" button on the video -> pick 4K / 1080p / 720p / MP3.
 - Normal downloads (exe, zip, pdf ...) go to FastDownloader automatically.
 - Tip: you can also just COPY a video link - FastDownloader shows the quality picker by itself.

Keep FastDownloader running (it sits in the tray, bottom-right) for the browser button to work.
